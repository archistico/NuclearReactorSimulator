# M10.9.4.1-E.3.2 Electrical Protection Evidence

**Status:** reviewed evidence and candidate threshold contract

**Evidence parent:** M10.9.4.1-E.3.1 Hotfix 1, locally compiled and fully tested by the user on 2026-07-26

## Evidence set

The user supplied the complete E.3.1 CSV and summary bundle generated by `scripts/run-electrical-protection-trajectory-audit.cmd`. The reviewed set contains:

- normal breaker-closed 5 -> 0 -> 5 MWe operation;
- breaker-closed turbine trip and sustained motoring/reverse power;
- breaker-open underfrequency coastdown;
- eight breaker-closed synthetic phase-offset trajectories from -135 degrees through +135 degrees.

The E.3.1 audit, ordinary suite and cumulative long-running gates all passed before threshold selection.

## Observed separation

### Reverse power

Normal load manoeuvring reached a minimum grid exchange of **-0.247872 MWe** for one sampled 0.1-second interval. The turbine-trip trajectory reached **-0.593835 MWe** and then remained near **-0.51 MWe** for approximately 30 seconds while the breaker remained closed.

The candidate contract therefore uses:

- pickup: **-0.30 MWe**;
- reset: **-0.10 MWe**;
- pickup delay: **2.0 s**;
- supervision: generator breaker closed;
- action: generator trip.

This rejects the isolated normal negative sample while retaining a wide duration margin over the sustained prime-mover-loss trajectory.

### Underfrequency

The normal load-step trajectory reached **48.720072 Hz**. Review of the CSV shows it remained at or below **48.8 Hz** for only about **0.6 s**. Every phase-offset trajectory remained at or below 48.8 Hz for approximately **4.5-4.6 s**. The disconnected coastdown fell to **42.989737 Hz**, but its breaker was open throughout and it correctly produced no generator-trip eligibility.

The candidate contract therefore uses:

- pickup: **48.8 Hz**;
- reset: **49.5 Hz**;
- pickup delay: **1.0 s**;
- supervision: generator breaker closed;
- action: generator trip.

### Loss of synchronism

The normal 5 -> 0 -> 5 MWe trajectory reached a maximum absolute frequency slip of **1.279928 Hz**. The phase-offset sweep reached maximum absolute slips between **2.770386 Hz** and **2.776667 Hz**. Review of the CSV shows every offset case remained at or above **1.5 Hz absolute slip** for approximately **4.3 s**.

Absolute phase angle alone is not a usable discriminator in the current reduced-order model: normal operation reached **179.437872 degrees**, while the synthetic offset cases reached approximately 176-179 degrees and repeatedly crossed the wrapped phase boundary. E.3.2 therefore uses sustained absolute frequency slip rather than a raw phase-angle threshold.

The candidate contract uses:

- pickup: **1.5 Hz absolute frequency slip**;
- reset: **0.5 Hz**;
- pickup delay: **0.5 s**;
- supervision: generator breaker closed;
- action: generator trip.

## Architectural consequences

The new functions remain canonical M5.5 measured-signal protection and are enabled in both current-v2 sustained profiles. E.3.2 adds generic optional pickup delay and measured supervision to `ProtectionFunctionDefinition`; legacy definitions retain zero delay and no supervision by default. Pickup elapsed time is committed protection state so pause/resume, replay and deterministic stepping do not depend on wall-clock time.

Breaker state and absolute generator/grid frequency slip are published through the instrumentation source catalog. No Application-layer relay is introduced. The focused E.3.2 gate also emits three concise implementation summaries and detailed CSV trajectories under `artifacts/e3-protection-implementation` so pickup timing and supervision evidence can be reviewed independently of the test verdict.

## Model boundary

These are educational reduced-order protection functions calibrated to the current-v2 10 MWe infinite-bus model. They are not claims of plant-grade relay coordination and do not introduce detailed synchronous-machine electromagnetic transients, excitation dynamics, impedance-based pole-slip detection or multi-machine network protection.
