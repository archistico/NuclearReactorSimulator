## Validated cumulative scope

A–D close seed, drum, condenser, turbine-admission, governor, passive-loss and valve-authority hardening. E.2 establishes the 10 MWe bidirectional current-v2 reference plant; E.3.1/E.3.2 add measured evidence and supervised delayed electrical protection. F.1 validates the compressible steam-flow capacity seam. F.2 validates one conservative atmospheric header-relief boundary.

The F.2 user-supplied audit confirmed first opening at 6.51 MPa, full lift at 6.70 MPa, 13.531762568 kg/s capacity at 6.80 MPa, 33.595745149 MW energy export, monotonicity and exact conservative external exchange.

## F.3 — Conservative Turbine Bypass to Condenser — CANDIDATE

F.3 adds one optional current-v2 pressure-actuated bypass from main-steam header `header` to condenser `condenser`, steam-space node `exhaust`:

```text
set pressure             6.400000 MPa
full-open pressure       6.500000 MPa
full-open throat area    1,600 mm²
```

The condenser system owns the topology. The solver reads committed source and destination pressure, uses the validated F.1 capacity law, blocks reverse flow, limits capacity by vapor availability and publishes equal/opposite internal mass and specific-internal-energy terms with zero external exchange. F.2 relief remains separate.

See ADR 0117, `M10_9_4_1_F3_TURBINE_BYPASS.md` and `M10_9_4_1_F3_VALIDATION_CHECKLIST.md`.
