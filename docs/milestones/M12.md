# M12 — Extreme Operations Foundations — PLAN

**Strategic epic:** Epic A — Extreme Operations & Accident Progression, foundation phase.  
**Status:** PLANNED — starts only after M11 release hardening is explicitly closed.

## Goal

Make the plant model intentionally robust and explicit at extreme/off-design states before granting broader accident/fault authority. M12 establishes physical support boundaries, credible post-trip heat ownership, persistent integrity primitives and incident-state persistence. It does **not** yet authorize severe consequence claims.

## Non-scope

M12 does not add scripted leaks/ruptures/fires, severe core-damage progression, UI-owned physics, new Instructor-only physical shortcuts or arbitrary protections whose only purpose is to keep the solver inside a comfortable range.

## M12.1 — Flow-owner directionality and support inventory

Audit every component that owns or constrains mass/energy flow and classify it explicitly as:

```text
BIDIRECTIONAL
ONE-WAY BY PHYSICS
ONE-WAY BY CHECK/ISOLATION DEVICE
UNSUPPORTED OUTSIDE CURRENT ENVELOPE
```

Acceptance requires definition-owned semantics, signed-flow regression coverage where supported, fail-closed behavior where unsupported and no global reverse-flow clamp.

## M12.2 — Extreme-state validation matrix

Exercise near-empty inventories and deliberately extreme but representable pressure, temperature, void/fill and flow states before fault authority is widened.

Acceptance requires deterministic bounded behavior, conservation/accounting evidence where applicable, explicit unsupported-envelope failures and no interlock added solely to hide numerical fragility.

## M12.3 — Full-plant post-trip decay-heat ownership

Integrate credible post-trip decay heat through the full-plant runtime and energy ownership chain. Existing decay-heat state remains distinct from immediate fission power.

Acceptance requires history-dependent post-trip heat, explicit energy ownership, replay/checkpoint equivalence and evidence that severe core-damage claims remain disabled until downstream thermal-state prerequisites exist.

## M12.4 — Persistent component integrity/stress primitives

Separate functional/effective state from physical integrity. Introduce deterministic definition-owned exposure/stress accumulation primitives suitable for later damage models.

Candidate integrity vocabulary may include `HEALTHY`, `STRESSED`, `DEGRADED`, `DAMAGED`, `FAILED`, `DESTROYED`, but exact states are frozen only after the contract is audited.

Acceptance requires that protection reset/alarm acknowledgement cannot silently repair physical integrity.

## M12.5 — Incident severity and persistence scaffolding

Introduce physical `IncidentSeverity` separately from alarm priority. Severity remains evidence-derived from modeled physical consequence and may remain `NONE` while alarms are critical if protection prevents damage.

Integrity/stress/severity state must be deterministic, checkpoint-compatible, replayable and visible to post-incident analysis without becoming a plant command owner.

## M12.6 — Extreme-foundation closure

Close M12 only after ordinary tests plus focused directionality/extreme-state/decay-heat/integrity/replay gates are green and the known-limitations register states which extreme conditions remain unsupported.

## Exit condition

M12 closes the **foundations only**. Leak, rupture, fire, mechanical/electrical failure and severe core-damage mechanisms remain M15 work and each requires its own explicit causal model.
