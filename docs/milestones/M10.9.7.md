## M10.9.7.3 — Objective/demand/progress/score UI

The workstation should answer, at a glance:

1. What am I trying to achieve?
2. What is the external demand now?
3. What load have I requested?
4. What is the plant actually producing?
5. How far am I from the target?
6. What safety/procedure/stability conditions are affecting my result?
7. What should I inspect next?

Use progressive disclosure rather than one dense dashboard.

### Acceptance

- demand/request/actual are never merged into one gauge/value;
- safety/protection significance remains visually dominant over score decoration;
- score decomposition remains understandable without color alone;
- no milestone/internal engineering labels leak into normal operator presentation.

## M10.9.7.4 — Deterministic performance timeline and drill-down

Provide a bounded logical-step timeline for mission events such as:

- objective/phase transition;
- demand change;
- key operator action;
- protection/alarm event relevant to scoring;
- score-dimension change;
- challenge completion/failure/protected-boundary classification.

Timeline ordering must reuse deterministic logical step/event sequence semantics. Wall clock may be displayed only as non-authoritative session metadata if already available; it may not order or score events.

Drill-down may navigate to the relevant subsystem schematic, command consequence or existing alarm/log evidence without mutating plant state.

## M10.9.7.5 — Closure gate
