# M15 — Accident Progression & Consequence Models — PLAN

**Strategic epic:** Epic A — Extreme Operations & Accident Progression, consequence phase.  
**Status:** PLANNED — prerequisites M12, M13 and M14 closed.

## Goal

Introduce persistent accident progression through explicit physical mechanisms rather than scripted flags. Each consequence family is independently modeled, deterministic, replayable and auditable before the next family is authorized.

## Hard prerequisites

- M12 extreme-envelope/directionality, decay-heat, integrity and incident-state foundations are validated;
- M13 supplies the operator/Instructor surfaces but owns no consequence physics;
- M14 supplies quasi-spatial evidence needed for any localized core-damage claims;
- no severe consequence may be inferred merely from alarm priority or a scenario label.

## M15.1 — Pressure-boundary damage

Model stress/exposure accumulation, integrity degradation, leak initiation/growth and rupture only where an explicit pressure-boundary failure criterion exists.

## M15.2 — Rotating-equipment degradation/failure

Model mechanical/thermal exposure, degradation and failure for supported pumps/turbine/generator equipment without generic `overspeed → broken` flags.

## M15.3 — Electrical damage and fire

Model electrical/thermal damage and fire only where a causal overload/arc/heating/ignition mechanism exists. Fire is not a generic consequence flag.

## M15.4 — Core-damage prerequisite gate and bounded progression

Before any core-damage implementation, prove that post-trip decay heat, inventory/cooling state and required local thermal evidence are sufficient. Only then introduce bounded uncovery/dryout/fuel-or-channel damage progression supported by the model.

## M15.5 — Physical incident severity

Derive `IncidentSeverity` from modeled consequence, affected equipment, integrity loss and persistence. Alarm priority remains an independent operator-attention axis.

## M15.6 — Persistent damage / replay / post-incident integration

Checkpoint, full replay and session archive must preserve/reconstruct initiating event, stress accumulation, damage transitions, leaks/ruptures/fires/failures, final integrity and severity. Ordinary protection reset may not repair physical damage.

## M15.7 — Instructor and spatial consequence presentation

Expose modeled consequences in Instructor/Fault mode, whole-plant mimics and M14 spatial layers without giving Avalonia any damage authority. Local visualization appears only for consequences whose localization is physically represented.

## M15.8 — Integrated accident closure

Run deterministic extreme-operation and recovery/failure matrices, replay/checkpoint equivalence, post-incident evidence, long-horizon performance and manual scenario review. Known unsupported severe phenomena must remain explicit limitations rather than scripted approximations.
