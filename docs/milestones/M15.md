# M15 — Accident Progression & Consequence Models — DETAILED PLAN

**Strategic epic:** Epic A — Extreme Operations & Accident Progression, consequence phase.  
**Status:** PLANNED — prerequisites M12, M13 and M14 closed.  
**Detailed cross-milestone execution map:** [`../FORWARD_EXECUTION_PLAN_M10_9_7_TO_M15.md`](../FORWARD_EXECUTION_PLAN_M10_9_7_TO_M15.md).

## Goal

Introduce persistent accident progression through explicit physical mechanisms rather than scripted flags. Each consequence family is independently modeled, deterministic, replayable and auditable before the next family is authorized.

## Hard prerequisites

- M12 extreme-envelope/directionality, decay-heat, integrity and incident-state foundations are validated;
- M13 supplies operator/Instructor surfaces but owns no consequence physics;
- M14 supplies quasi-spatial evidence needed for localized core-damage claims;
- no severe consequence may be inferred merely from alarm priority or a scenario label.

## Universal consequence contract

Every implemented consequence family must identify:

1. initiating physical condition;
2. exposure/stress accumulator or direct failure criterion;
3. persistent integrity/damage owner;
4. deterministic transition rule;
5. resulting physical source/sink/constraint/change;
6. instrumentation/alarm/protection evidence;
7. operator/instructor observability;
8. checkpoint/replay/session persistence;
9. post-incident reconstruction;
10. explicit fidelity limitations.

No consequence is implemented as `if scenario flag then damaged`.

---

## M15.1 — Pressure-boundary damage, leak and rupture

### Candidate supported owners

Start only with pressure boundaries whose geometry/state/pressure exposure are explicit enough to justify a criterion, e.g. selected:

- pipe/line segments;
- headers;
- steam lines;
- steam-drum boundary abstractions;
- heat-exchanger/condenser boundaries only if the model exposes enough state.

### Progression

A possible generic progression is:

```text
NORMAL
→ OVERSTRESS_EXPOSURE
→ DEGRADED
→ LEAKING
→ RUPTURED
```

Exact states/thresholds are component-specific and must be validated; do not force one law onto every boundary.

### Physical effect

Leak/rupture must create real canonical mass/energy source terms consistent with M12 directionality and conservation. Rupture is not just a larger alarm.

### Tests

- below-limit no damage;
- exposure accumulation/recovery semantics;
- leak onset deterministic;
- leak mass/energy balance;
- isolation interaction;
- protection response;
- replay/checkpoint/session identity;
- no ordinary reset repairs the boundary.

---

## M15.2 — Rotating-equipment degradation/failure

### Candidate mechanisms

Only implement mechanisms the existing model can support with credible evidence, such as:

- turbine/generator overspeed exposure;
- pump dry-running/low-inventory exposure if hydraulically represented;
- persistent thermal overload;
- electrical/mechanical load exposure where canonical quantities exist.

### State separation

A component may be:

- functionally tripped but healthy;
- running while degraded;
- failed/damaged and therefore unavailable;
- isolated after failure.

### Effects

Failure must alter the owning component's capability/parameters through explicit state, not via UI or scenario script.

### Qualification

Include healthy controls proving ordinary trips do not create damage.

---

## M15.3 — Electrical damage and fire

### Staged approach

Do not start with a generic `FIRE` fault. Build a causal chain such as:

```text
electrical overload / fault current / thermal exposure
→ equipment/insulation heating or damage
→ ignition eligibility
→ active fire
→ persistent affected-equipment/environment consequence
```

Only links supported by modeled quantities should exist.

### Fire state

If introduced, fire must be persistent physical incident state with:

- location/equipment owner;
- intensity/progression representation appropriate to fidelity;
- effects explicitly scoped;
- alarm/instrument evidence;
- deterministic replay/session persistence.

### Non-scope until separately modeled

- arbitrary smoke propagation;
- human evacuation;
- generalized building fire CFD;
- fire causing unrelated equipment to fail without a modeled coupling.

---

## M15.4 — Core-damage prerequisite gate

### Purpose

Prove the model can represent the entire causal chain before any core-damage code is authorized.

Required chain:

```text
fission shutdown
→ validated decay heat
→ coolant inventory / cooling effectiveness
→ local or zone thermal state
→ uncovery/dryout/insufficient heat removal evidence
→ physically meaningful damage criterion
```

### Gate output

For every required quantity state:

- canonical owner;
- units;
- spatial resolution;
- update order;
- replay/checkpoint source;
- known fidelity limitation.

If one necessary link is missing, M15.5 remains blocked and the missing physics gets its own scoped work.

---

## M15.5 — Bounded core-damage progression

### Fidelity principle

Implement only reduced-order damage states supported by M12/M14 evidence. Potential states might include:

- local/group uncovery;
- dryout/insufficient heat removal;
- fuel/channel overtemperature exposure;
- degraded/damaged fuel-channel-group state;
- limited release/source-term placeholder **only if** a physically owned quantity exists — otherwise explicitly defer it.

### Requirements

- local/group damage cannot appear without local/group prerequisite evidence;
- aggregate core state reflects local damage consistently;
- protection may prevent or reduce progression but cannot erase damage already accumulated;
- damage is not challenge/scenario-owned.

### Non-scope unless separately modeled

- detailed fuel melt relocation;
- corium chemistry;
- pressure-vessel melt-through if the modeled reactor concept/plant abstraction cannot support it;
- atmospheric dispersion/radiological dose claims without a dedicated source-term/transport model.

---

## M15.6 — Physical incident severity and post-incident integration

### Severity derivation

Use modeled evidence such as:

- integrity state;
- number/importance of affected systems;
- persistent leak/fire/damage;
- local/core damage extent where modeled;
- loss of safety functions where the simulator actually represents them.

Alarm priority remains independent.

### Post-incident timeline

Must reconstruct:

1. initiating fault/event;
2. first abnormal exposure;
3. protection/operator response;
4. first integrity transition;
5. subsequent leak/rupture/fire/failure transitions;
6. peak consequence evidence;
7. final stable/recovered/degraded state;
8. final incident severity.

Do not claim causal inference beyond the explicit model chain.

---

## M15.7 — Instructor and spatial consequence presentation

### Instructor

Instructor mode may trigger supported initiating faults and observe progression. It may not set `Damaged=true` directly unless that is explicitly an instructor-only test seam not exposed as gameplay and never confused with physical simulation; preferred behavior is always to initiate the modeled cause.

### Whole-plant HMI

Expose:

- persistent damaged/degraded equipment;
- leak/rupture/fire location;
- isolation status;
- relevant alarms/protection;
- physical incident severity.

### Spatial HMI

M14 map may show local consequences only when localization exists in the physical owner. No decorative red zones with no model state.

---

## M15.8 — Integrated accident closure

### Scenario families

Build a bounded matrix including healthy controls and representative failures:

- pressure-boundary leak with successful isolation;
- escalating leak/rupture where supported;
- rotating-equipment failure;
- electrical damage/fire case;
- post-trip decay-heat cooling success;
- degraded cooling trajectory;
- core-damage progression only if M15.4 passed;
- recovery to stable but damaged state;
- unrecovered persistent incident state.

### Automated evidence

- conservation/source-term accounting;
- deterministic repeat;
- full replay;
- checkpoint seek/resume;
- session archive round-trip;
- integrity/severity persistence;
- post-incident timeline identity;
- long-horizon performance/memory;
- protection/command authority invariants.

### Manual review

- accident progression understandable but not sensationalized;
- Instructor/operator distinction clear;
- damage persists appropriately;
- unsupported severe phenomena explicitly identified;
- spatial display does not overclaim fidelity.

### Exit condition

M15 closes only when every enabled severe consequence is traceable to a validated physical mechanism. Anything else remains an explicit known limitation or future milestone.
